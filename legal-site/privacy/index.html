Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the Estimation card game processes, why it is used, and the choices available to players.

Effective: 31 August 2026 · Developer: Hope TV · Package: com.mostafaazab.estimation

Short version

Estimation uses Supabase for accounts and online game data and Google for optional sign-in. We do not sell personal data. Other players can see the display name and profile image you choose to use in multiplayer. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Request account deletion Email privacy support

1. Data we process

  • Account information: Supabase user ID and, when Google sign-in is used, email address and basic Google profile information.
  • Player profile: display name, selected or uploaded profile image, rank, level, XP, games played, and games won.
  • Game activity: room membership, game status, scores, match history, and information required to synchronize an online match.
  • Local preferences: audio, haptic, visual, profile, and gameplay preferences stored on the device.
  • Network information: local-network addresses and discovery information processed when players choose local multiplayer. This is used to connect devices and is not intended for advertising.
  • Photos: a photo is accessed only when you choose one for your profile. You can use a preset avatar instead.

2. Why we use data

  • To authenticate players and synchronize profiles across devices.
  • To create, join, operate, reconnect to, and score multiplayer games.
  • To provide history, progression, ranks, and leaderboards.
  • To save preferences and provide support, security, and abuse prevention.

3. Service providers and other players

We use Google Sign-In for optional authentication and Supabase for authentication, database, and realtime services. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. In multiplayer, your chosen display name, avatar, and game-visible status or score may be shown to other participants. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Account profile and game-history data are retained while the account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion from the app or from our account-deletion page. We delete or de-identify account-linked data unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. Residual encrypted backup copies may remain for a limited recovery period before expiration.

5. Security and choices

We use encrypted network transport for cloud services and access controls intended to restrict account data. No system is completely secure. You can play without linking Google where anonymous/local play is supported, choose a preset avatar, sign out, or request account deletion.

6. Children and changes

Parents or guardians who believe a child supplied personal information may contact us to request review or deletion. We may update this policy when the game or its service providers change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or rights requests, email privacy@hope-tv.site. Account-deletion requests should follow the verification steps on the deletion page.
