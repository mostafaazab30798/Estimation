Community guidelines

By using display names or custom profile photos in Estimation, you agree to these rules.

App: Estimation · Package: com.mostafaazab.estimation · Version: 2026-08-31

Allowed content

  • Display names between 2 and 20 characters using letters, numbers, spaces, and basic punctuation.
  • Preset avatars provided by the app, or a gallery photo that is appropriate for all ages.
  • Respectful interaction with other players.

Not allowed

  • Hate speech, harassment, sexual content, violence, impersonation, or illegal material in names or photos.
  • Sharing another person’s private information.
  • Attempting to bypass blocks or moderation.

Report and block

Players can report another user from the leaderboard (long-press a row) or from in-game player cards. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours. Blocking hides a player from your leaderboard and interactions.

We may remove content, suspend features, or terminate accounts that violate these guidelines.
