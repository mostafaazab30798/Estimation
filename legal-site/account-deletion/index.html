Delete your account

This page lets Estimation players request deletion without having the app installed.

App: Estimation · Developer: Hope TV · Package: com.mostafaazab.estimation

Delete inside the app (recommended)

If Estimation is installed and you are signed in with Google: open Profile → Settings → Account and privacy → Delete account. Confirm by typing DELETE and re-authenticating with Google. Deletion is immediate for the auth account and associated profile data.

Delete without the app

  1. Email us from the same Google email address connected to your Estimation account.
  2. Keep the pre-filled subject and include your in-game display name if known.
  3. We may ask for limited additional account information to prevent unauthorized deletion. Never send a password, access token, or identity document.
  4. After verification, the account and deletable associated data will be removed. We will confirm completion by email.
Email deletion request Read the privacy policy

Before requesting deletion

Deletion is permanent. Progress, XP, ranks, cloud profile information, and match history cannot be restored after the request is completed. Removing the app from a device does not delete the cloud account.

Data deleted

  • The Supabase authentication account associated with the verified request.
  • Email, user ID, display name, profile image, XP, level, wins, and games-played profile data.
  • Account-linked match history and room membership records.
  • Other account-linked data that is not required to be retained.

Data that may be retained

Limited records may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. De-identified aggregate statistics that can no longer identify the account may also remain. Residual encrypted backups may persist for a limited recovery period before expiration.

In-app route

When the app is installed, open Profile → Settings → Account and privacy → Delete account for immediate deletion after Google verification. The web email option below remains available without installing the app.

طلب حذف حساب «إستميشن»

  1. أرسل الطلب من نفس بريد Google المرتبط بحسابك في اللعبة.
  2. اذكر اسمك داخل اللعبة إن كان معروفاً.
  3. قد نطلب معلومات محدودة للتحقق ومنع حذف حساب شخص آخر. لا ترسل كلمة المرور أو رمز الدخول أو مستند هوية.
  4. بعد التحقق، نحذف الحساب والبيانات القابلة للحذف ونرسل لك تأكيداً عبر البريد.

الحذف نهائي ويشمل الملف الشخصي والتقدم والإحصائيات وسجل المباريات المرتبط بالحساب. حذف التطبيق من الهاتف وحده لا يحذف الحساب السحابي.
